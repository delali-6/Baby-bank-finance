# Baby Bank Finance — User Guide

This guide covers the day-to-day use of Baby Bank Finance. The screens and
actions available to you depend on whether your account is a **staff** account
or an **administrator** account.

## 1. Sign in and manage your account

1. Open the Baby Bank Finance web address provided by your charity.
2. Enter your registered email address and password, then select **Sign in**.
3. If you have forgotten your password, select **Forgot password?**, enter your
   email address, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox.
4. To finish a session, open **Profile** and select **Log out**.

### Register a new account

1. On the sign-in screen, select **Create an account**.
2. Enter your full name, email address, and a password between 12 and 128
   characters. Confirm the password and submit the form.
3. Check your email and confirm your address using the link sent to you.
4. After confirming your email, wait for an administrator to approve your
   access. Until then, financial records remain unavailable. Contact your
   charity administrator if approval is delayed or you think it was made in
   error.

You can view your name, email, role, and access status on **Profile**.

## 2. Dashboard

The **Dashboard** gives an overview of recorded income, expenditure, and
balance, plus a weekly summary and recent transactions. Use **Previous** and
**Next** to change the week shown. Select **Record transaction** to add a
record, or **View all transactions** to open the full history.

## 3. Record a transaction

1. Open **Transactions** or select **Record transaction** from the dashboard.
2. Choose **Income** for money received or **Expenditure** for money spent.
3. Enter the transaction date and amount in pounds (£), then select an active
   category for that type.
4. Add a clear description. A reference, such as a receipt number, is optional.
5. Check the details and select **Save transaction**. Wait for the success
   message before leaving the page.

The amount must be greater than £0.00 and use no more than two decimal places.
Descriptions must have at least three characters. Transactions are not saved
while offline; reconnect and submit again if you see a connection error. If no
categories are available, contact an administrator.

## 4. Search transaction history

Open **History** to review records. You can:

- Search by words in the transaction description using the search box.
- Switch between **All transactions** and **Group by week**.
- Review date, type, category, description, reference, status, and amount.

Voided records do not count toward financial totals. Administrators can see
voided records and their recorded reason in history; staff see active records.
Staff cannot edit or void transactions from this screen.

## 5. Financial reports

Open **Reports** to view income, expenditure, and net balance, along with
weekly summaries and a monthly trend chart. To report on a specific period,
choose **From** and/or **To** dates and select **Apply dates**. Select **Clear**
to return to the full date range. The start date must be on or before the end
date.

Select **Export PDF** to download a report with the selected period, summary
totals, weekly and monthly summaries, and detailed transaction records.
Voided transactions are excluded from report totals and exports.

## 6. Administration

Administrator-only links appear in the workspace navigation.

### Approve and manage users

1. Open **User Management**.
2. Review the registered users and their access status.
3. Select **Approve** for a pending staff account, or **Deactivate** to remove
   an active staff member's access. An inactive account can be approved again.

An administrator cannot change their own access here, and administrator
accounts are managed separately. Only approve people who should have access to
charity financial records.

### Charity settings

Open **Settings** to view the charity name, currency, timezone, and first day
of the week. Administrators can change these values and save them; staff can
view the settings but cannot edit them. Agree changes with the charity before
saving, as these preferences affect how financial information is presented.
Settings changes are recorded in the audit log.

### Void or restore transactions

Administrators can use **History** to void an active transaction. Select
**Void**, enter a reason, and confirm. Voiding removes the transaction from
financial totals without erasing its history. To include it in totals again,
select **Restore** on the voided record and confirm.

Take care when voiding or restoring records: the change affects dashboard and
report totals. The reason is required when voiding a transaction.

### Audit log

Open **Audit Log** to review the latest recorded activity, including who made
changes and the previous or new data where available. The page shows up to the
100 most recent entries.

## Getting help

For approval, access, missing categories, or questions about a financial entry,
contact your charity administrator. If a page reports that information could
not be loaded, refresh it and try again; contact an administrator if the
problem continues.
